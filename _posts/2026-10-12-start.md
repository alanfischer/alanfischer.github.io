Although the signal to noise ratio here will be low it will be greater than zero...

I've been having fun recreating an old half-life mod in [Godot](https://godotengine.org/) and in the process have spun out a few separate libraries:
- [goldsrc-godot](https://github.com/alanfischer/goldsrc-godot) A GDExtension to load goldsrc resources (bsp, mdl, spr, wad).  Part of the fun here was implementing [PVS Runtime Visibility Culling](https://github.com/alanfischer/goldsrc-godot#pvs-runtime-visibility-culling) and [BSP Hull Collision](https://github.com/alanfischer/goldsrc-godot#goldsrc-hull-collision).  Although you can't implement the same exact goldsrc hull collision without a custom physics engine...
- [hop-godot](https://github.com/alanfischer/hop-godot) A GDExtension that uses the [hop](https://github.com/alanfischer/hop) physics engine (A standalone header-only fixed & floating point C++17 physics library for swept-collision simulation) which lets us implement the [Goldsrc BSP Hull Collision](https://github.com/alanfischer/hop-godot/blob/main/README.md#goldsrc-bsp-hull-collision).  However you also really want the low bandwidth netcode...
- [goldnet-godot](https://github.com/alanfischer/goldnet-godot) A GDExtension that implements goldsrc style netcode - an unreliable, delta-against-acked-baseline snapshot stream.  It's almost a drop-in replacement for a MultiplayerSynchronizer.

Had a fairly fun gaming session last night with surprisingly few bugs...
