Day 3 began suddenly - our tucked away tree line that was so well hidden on a Friday night turned out to be right by a horde of parents dropping their kids off for a bus - on a Saturday!

I quickly woke Ardan & Tom and we packed up as discreetly as one can pack up hammocks, under quilts, sleeping bags, and clothes on to a bike.  I'm unsure if the parents to the west of us and the police car to the east of us didn't notice us, didn't care, or didn't get to us in time.

We rolled out of there and quickly headed to the first restaurant we could find for breakfast - The Red Rock Cafe.  Their biscuits and gravy were fantastic.  We also were not the first racers to have gone through there!  The waitress wanted to know our names so she could follow us on trackleaders.

From there we started working our way out west on New Haven Road to curl our way in the general direction of Devils Tower.

Our intricate plan for tackling the traversal of the tower hinged on one key element - ice cream.  We found this sign at one of the shops right at the park entrance...

"1 SCOOP - 4$.  2 SCOOPS - 7$"

We opted for 2 scoops.  Besides, we had earned a couple scoops of ice cream!

It turned out 2 was more like 7.  Maybe the dollar sign was on the wrong number?  Either way, it worked out in our favor.  No ice cream in our bowls survived very long.

From there we rolled onward into the park.  It was a fantastic September day, with more people trying to get into the park than probably live in the entire state.  The queue to enter the park extended for some distance... but it did not extend to us!  We casually peddled our way along the shoulder, leapfrogging the cars.

Finally, we parked our bikes and started the hike around the tower.
